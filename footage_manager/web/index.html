Your review workflow

  1. Review a recording. Jump, scrub, speed up playback, or load storyboard previews. Your position saves automatically.
  2. Mark moments. Set IN and OUT, then choose Keep, Maybe / later, Re-review, or Reject. Titles, tags, and collections help you find them later.
  3. Extract and decide. Extract keepers, check them, then mark the recording Keep full, Keep clips, or Delete. Trash first; permanent deletion reclaims space.

Use All moments to collect clips across sessions. Filter by collection, intended format, or usage. “In a project” and “Published” help avoid reusing clips accidentally.

Space: play/pause · arrows: ±10 seconds · Shift+arrows: ±1 second · comma/period: step frames · I/O: mark start/end · K/M/R/X: save a keep/later/re-review/reject range.

Shortcuts stay inside this app and do not affect your streaming hotkeys. Typing in notes or time fields does not trigger them.

Maybe and re-review ranges block source deletion. Trash keeps using disk space until you delete it permanently. Backups save your catalogue, not the videos.
