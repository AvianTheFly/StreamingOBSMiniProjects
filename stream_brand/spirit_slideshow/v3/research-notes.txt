RESEARCH BEHIND THE CALMER EDIT

Adobe - What is continuity editing in film?
https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/video/hub/ideas/what-is-continuity-editing-in-film.html
Continuity establishes time and space. Screen direction, match on action and eye
trace help viewers follow events across cuts. Applied here: reduce independent
pose substitutions; preserve the ram POV until the fall; allow more time to read
each location and action. The 1.2-second minimum is an authored choice, not an
industry rule or a scientifically established comfort threshold.

Adobe - Sequence shots
https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/video/production/cinematography/camera-shots-and-angles/sequence-shot.html
Maintaining consistent spatial relationships avoids confusing layout jumps.
Applied here: remove charge overhead and reverse fall POV inserts; keep the
bridge footfall and failure before the exterior fall.

Adobe - Over-the-shoulder shot
https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud/video/production/cinematography/camera-shots-and-angles/over-the-shoulder-shot.html
Eyelines and consistent lighting help adjacent shots read as one scene.
Applied here: avoid rapid repeated switches among rear, frontal, side and POV
views; use a restrained consistent video grade rather than alternating glare.

W3C - Understanding SC 2.3.3: Animation from Interactions
https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/animation-from-interactions
Unnecessary motion and parallax can produce discomfort for some viewers.
Applied here: no added moving crops, shakes, spins or zoom pulses; preview does
not autoplay. This criterion concerns web interactions. It is design guidance
here, not a formal compliance claim for this movie.

W3C - Multimedia and Animations designer curriculum
https://www.w3.org/WAI/curricula/designer-modules/multimedia-and-animations/
Avoid unnecessary flashing. Applied here: remove abrupt micro-shot switching,
use short opacity transitions and reduce video highlights. Pixel-change numbers
are descriptive comparisons, not a formal photosensitive-flash assessment.

The transition method is an artistic choice. Short dissolves ease abrupt changes
between independently generated paintings but cannot create physically exact
continuous character animation. Actual transition strips must be inspected for
ghosted outlines and confusing overlaps before delivery.
